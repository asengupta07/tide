# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DeFi users, liquidity providers, protocol developers, and hackathon evaluators using Tide to understand, create, and monitor partially active AMM strategies. They need to grasp an unfamiliar mechanism quickly, verify its on-chain behavior, and operate strategies without losing sight of custody or risk.

## Product Purpose

Tide demonstrates and operates a partially active AMM that exposes only a controlled fraction of maker inventory per block while offering deeper virtual pricing to later flow. Success means users can understand the mechanism, inspect live proof, create a strategy, and manage it confidently through ENS and World ID-backed controls.

## Brand Personality

Confident, technical, cinematic. The interface should feel precise and research-backed without becoming cold, obscure, or intimidating.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page patterns, cramped crypto trading terminals, decorative Web3 neon, gratuitous glass panels, and tiny typography that mistakes low density for sophistication.

## Design Principles

- Make the unfamiliar mechanism legible before asking users to trust it.
- Show custody, constraints, and live on-chain proof at the point of decision.
- Use cinematic atmosphere to create identity, while keeping product controls direct and calm.
- Preserve useful information density without making text, controls, or targets feel miniature.
- Let the interface scale fluidly across laptops and desktops instead of assuming one browser zoom or device scale.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and interaction standards. Maintain keyboard-visible controls, readable body text, adequate touch targets, reduced-motion alternatives, and layouts that remain usable under browser zoom, operating-system display scaling, and narrow viewports.
